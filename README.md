The twist for Moonfall life:
  Each player starts on 6 lives, dark green if you have 4 or more, green if you have 3, yellow if you have 2 and once you're on your last life, you'll be red. Yellows can kill dark greens and reds can kill anyone they wish.
  This season, there will be a different moon every night. These moons could have either positive or negative effects that alter your gameplay. 
  Additionally, there is a 1 in 5 chance that a moon will be a blood moon, amplifying these effects.
  There is the boogeyman that is selected each session and is forced to kill someone before the end or else they turn red.
